Small business, nonprofit & government resources

Kyro also has self-paced learning resources you can start from Nexus (ask to "learn about grant writing", "financial literacy", "marketing strategy", "minority-owned business development", "government partnership readiness", or "technology modernization"). These cover:

  • Small business financial literacy: cash flow, budgeting, bookkeeping, and funding options.
  • Marketing strategy: audience, positioning, channel plan, and measurement.
  • Grant writing for nonprofits: funder fit, case for support, budget narrative, and a submission checklist.
  • Minority-owned business development: readiness for programs such as the SBA 8(a) Business Development Program, the Minority Business Development Agency (MBDA), and state/local MBE or DBE certification.
  • Government & public-sector partnership readiness: stakeholder mapping, curriculum and training design, and a pilot-then-scale rollout plan.
  • Technology modernization planning for organizations with limited budget or connectivity.

These are education and planning-template resources only. Always verify current program rules, deadlines, and eligibility directly on each program's own official site before applying.
