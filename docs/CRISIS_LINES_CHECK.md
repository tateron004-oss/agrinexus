# Crisis lines for Kenya and Nigeria: what was checked (13 October 2026)

Used in the caring crisis reply (`public/kyro-crisis-lines.js`). No government-run suicide crisis line could be confirmed in either country, so none is named; these are charities, and the reply says what each page said about hours.

## Used
- **Befrienders Kenya, +254 722 178 177**: weekdays 9 am to 5 pm (not 24 hours). Seen on Lifeline International's Befrienders Kenya page and on findahelpline (Kenya, suicidal thoughts). Not toll-free as far as any page said.
- **Kenya Red Cross toll-free line, 1199**: shown as "Toll Free: 1199" on the Red Cross's own contact page (dated 2023 only). That page does not say it is for mental health; third-party pages (MHPSS hub, hapakenya 2026) say it is a counselling/mental health line, one says only for disaster response. The reply therefore says "where you can ask for counselling". Re-check on a newer official page.
- **SURPIN (Nigeria), 0800 078 7746**: toll-free and 24 hours per Lifeline International's SURPIN page; the same number is on SURPIN's own site (surpinng.com) and findahelpline. A charity, not government.

## Seen but NOT used
- Kenya: 0800 723 253 (labelled three different ways); 1190 (LVCT/one2one, general helpline, hours unconfirmed); 0776543099 (Mental 360, one page only); +254 793594849 and +254 754580252 (search snippet only); Befrienders hours of 9 to 5 seven days (conflicts with weekdays).
- Nigeria: MANI numbers (three different sets on different pages); 0809 210 6493 (unclear owner); Lagos Lifeline 07000006463 / 02014106463 / 09090006463 (Lagos State only, and not toll-free on the government's own account); 2017 Lagos numbers (old); 999, 998 and 122 as emergency numbers (no official page).
- Ghana, Uganda and Tanzania lines were found (Ghana Mental Health Authority 0800 678 678; Uganda Mental Health Uganda 0800 21 21 21; Tanzania Ministry of Health 199) but the platform does not serve those countries yet, so they are not in the reply. Ghana's Mental Health Authority line is the best-supported government line found anywhere in this check.

## Emergency numbers
Kenya 999 or 112 and Nigeria 112 (already in `nexus/companion/safety.js`) are consistent with what was found (a Canadian government travel page for Kenya and Nigeria; Nigerian news coverage of the 112 decision, April and July 2026). Nigeria's 199 may also work; it is not used.
